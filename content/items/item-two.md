Monetization depends on high-quality indexation, visitor retention, and seamless ad delivery. Static architecture gives your ad tags a reliable foundation.

## Preserving Core Web Vitals With Ad Slots

Ad units often introduce abrupt shifts when dynamic script tags insert unreserved containers into the layout.

- **Explicit Dimensions**: Always allocate fixed aspect ratio slots in your CSS grid before ad scripts run.
- **Semantic Structure**: Wrap primary editorial content within explicit landmark tags like `main` and `article`.
- **Fast Crawling**: When crawlers inspect your site, zero delay guarantees higher organic search positioning and better ad bids.
