The Sub-200-Line Codebase Rule

Modular simplicity that keeps your codebase clean and AI-friendly.

Software systems deteriorate when individual files grow into unmanageable monoliths. Imposing a strict 200-line limit forces disciplined architecture.

Why 200 Lines Matters

When files stay concise, every module has a single clear purpose.

  • Fast Cognitive Load: Developers can review and understand an entire file in under two minutes.
  • AI Pairing Resilience: Automated coding tools and language models maintain higher accuracy without context saturation.
  • Easy Testing: Compact functions with explicit inputs and outputs are trivial to verify and refactor.
